Our Philosophy

Fashion with a
conscience.

Noir Thread was born in 2018 from a simple conviction — that beautiful clothes should not cost the earth. Every piece is designed in Mumbai, made in verified ethical workshops, and crafted to outlast fast fashion.

We use only GOTS-certified organic cotton, deadstock fabrics, and OEKO-TEX approved dyes across every collection.

Ethical Sourcing
Verified supplier chain, fair wages, safe workspaces
Slow Fashion
Seasonal drops, never overproduced, never discarded
Natural Fibres
Linen, cotton, silk, TENCEL — nothing synthetic
Carbon Neutral
B Corp certified, carbon neutral since 2023
